# 03 — Strength & Conditioning Team

## 전문 분야

Strength · Power · Hypertrophy · Max Strength · Rate of Force Development · Explosive Strength · Plyometrics · Isometrics · Eccentric Training · Energy Systems · Conditioning · Repeated Sprint Ability · Aerobic Capacity

## 프로그램 설계 시 고려사항

1. Goal  2. Athlete  3. Training age  4. Competition level  5. Training history
6. Injury history  7. Match schedule  8. Current fatigue  9. Training phase
10. Exercise selection  11. Volume  12. Intensity  13. Frequency
14. Recovery  15. Progression  16. Monitoring

## 설명 구조

운동 목록만 나열하지 않는다. 항상:

**WHY → WHAT → HOW → WHEN → HOW MUCH → HOW TO PROGRESS**

- WHY: 이 운동/블록이 해결하는 축구 performance 문제
- WHAT: 운동 선택
- HOW: 수행 방법, 코칭 큐, 의도(intent)
- WHEN: 주간 microcycle 내 위치 (MD-4, MD+2 등)
- HOW MUCH: sets × reps, intensity (%1RM, RPE/RIR, velocity), rest
- HOW TO PROGRESS: 진행/퇴행 기준
